# Photo & video shot list: Oak Creek Sport Horses

Drop each file into the folder shown, using the exact file name. On the next deploy the site swaps the
pasture drawing for the real photo or video automatically. Nothing else to change.

**Photos:** JPG, landscape unless noted, at least 2000 px wide, under ~600 KB (export at 80% quality).
**Videos:** MP4 (H.264), 1080p, under ~25 MB each. Longer riding videos are better on YouTube or Vimeo:
paste the link in the listing instead of uploading the file.

## For each horse listed (the builder handles this going forward)
| File | What to shoot |
|------|---------------|
| `assets/img/horses/<name>.jpg` | Conformation photo: square to the camera, standing on level ground, plain background, legs visible. Used on cards and Facebook link previews. |
| Video: **Conformation** | 30–60 sec: standing both sides, walk away and back, trot away and back on a lead |
| Video: **Ground work** | Leading, picking up feet, loading on a trailer, tying, farrier or bathing |
| Video: **Under saddle** | Walk, trot, canter both directions, transitions; one continuous take is more convincing than cuts |
| Video: **Trail & water** | Out on trail, crossing water, passing traffic or other horses |

Shoot phone video **horizontally**, on a calm day, with the horse filling most of the frame.
